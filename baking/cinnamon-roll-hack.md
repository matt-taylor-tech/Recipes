# Heavy Cream Cinnamon Roll Hack

**Makes:** 5 rolls (one 8x8 or 9-inch glass dish) · **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 30 min
**Tags:** baking, breakfast, shortcut
**Source:** The viral canned-cinnamon-roll hack

## Ingredients

- 1 can (17.5 oz) jumbo refrigerated cinnamon rolls (like Pillsbury Grands), icing cup set aside
- 1/2 cup heavy cream, warmed slightly
- 4 tbsp butter, melted
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Butter a glass 8x8 baking dish or 9-inch glass pie plate.
2. Pour the **warm heavy cream into the bottom of the dish** so it covers the whole floor of the glass.
3. Set the rolls in the cream, spaced slightly apart. Let them sit **10 minutes** to soak up some cream and start to rise.
4. Stir the melted butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon together and spoon it over and between the rolls.
5. Bake **25–30 minutes**, until puffed, golden, and cooked through in the center. Tent loosely with foil if the tops brown before the middles are done.
6. Let rest **5 minutes** so the cream-and-sugar layer on the bottom thickens into caramel, then spread the icing over the warm rolls.

## Notes

- The cream on the bottom is the whole hack: the rolls soak it up and turn soft and gooey like bakery rolls, and it cooks down with the brown sugar into a caramel sauce.
- Glass lets you check the bottoms; when the sauce is bubbling and amber around the edges, they're done.
- Double everything for 2 cans in a 9x13 glass dish; add about **5 minutes** to the bake.
