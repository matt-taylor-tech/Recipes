# Rich Banana Bread

**Makes:** 1 loaf (9x5 pan) · **Prep:** 20 min · **Cook:** 60 min
**Tags:** baking, quick-bread
**Source:** [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/17066/janets-rich-banana-bread/)

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 1 cup white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts
- 2 medium bananas, mashed

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
2. Stir the melted butter and sugar together. Mix in the eggs and vanilla.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt. Stir into the butter mixture until just smooth.
4. Fold in the sour cream, walnuts, and bananas.
5. Spread the batter into the pan.
6. Bake about 60 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
7. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.
