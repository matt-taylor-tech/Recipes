# Mustard-Based BBQ Sauce

**Makes:** 1 1/2 cups · **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 10 min
**Tags:** sauces, bbq
**Source:** Allrecipes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup prepared yellow mustard
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup white vinegar
- Ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Stir the mustard, honey, brown sugar, and vinegar together in a saucepan over medium heat. Season with black pepper.
2. Bring to a boil, then simmer, stirring, for 5 minutes.

## Notes

- Brush on grilled ribs, or pour over cooked pulled pork and heat through.
