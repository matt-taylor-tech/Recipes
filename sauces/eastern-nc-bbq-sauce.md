# Eastern North Carolina BBQ Sauce

**Makes:** 2 cups · **Prep:** 5 min · **Chill:** 1 day
**Tags:** sauces, bbq, no-cook
**Source:** Allrecipes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup white vinegar
- 1 cup cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tbsp hot pepper sauce (e.g. Tabasco), or to taste
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Combine all the ingredients in a jar or bottle with a tight-fitting lid and shake well.
2. Refrigerate, shaking occasionally, until the flavors blend, 1 to 2 days.

## Notes

- On the hot side. Cut back the cayenne and hot sauce for a milder sauce.
- Keeps in the refrigerator for up to 2 months.
