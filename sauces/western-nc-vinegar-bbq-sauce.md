# Western North Carolina Vinegar Barbeque Sauce

**Makes:** 1 quart · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 35 min · **Chill:** 2 days
**Tags:** sauces, bbq
**Source:** Allrecipes

## Ingredients

- 4 cups cider vinegar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 1/3 cups ketchup
- 1/4 cup butter
- 2 tbsp hot pepper sauce (e.g. Tabasco)
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- 2 tsp mustard powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp fresh ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Put all the ingredients in a saucepan. Bring to a simmer over high heat, then reduce to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 30 minutes.
2. Pour into a heat-proof bowl. Cover and refrigerate for 2 days.
3. Strain through a mesh sieve to remove the red pepper flakes.
4. Store in the refrigerator. Bring to room temperature before serving.

## Notes

- Keep some in a squeeze bottle for serving and the rest in a glass quart jar in the fridge.
- To take the chill off quickly, warm it in the microwave.
