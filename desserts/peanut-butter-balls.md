# Peanut Butter Balls

**Makes:** 4 dozen balls · **Prep:** 30 min · **Cook:** 5 min · **Chill:** 3 hr
**Tags:** desserts, no-bake, christmas
**Source:** Allrecipes

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cups creamy peanut butter
- 1/2 cup butter
- 4 cups confectioners' sugar
- 3 cups crisp rice cereal
- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Melt the peanut butter and butter together in a medium saucepan over low heat, stirring often.
2. Mix the confectioners' sugar and cereal in a large bowl until well combined. Pour in the peanut butter mixture and stir until well combined. The mixture will be crumbly but will hold together when pressed.
3. Form into 1-inch balls and place on parchment-lined cookie sheets. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate until firm, 3 hours to overnight.
4. Melt the chocolate in a double boiler over low heat.
5. Drop 1 peanut butter ball into the chocolate and roll it with 2 forks to coat. Let the excess drip back into the pot, then place on a wire rack set over parchment paper. Repeat with the remaining balls.
6. Refrigerate until firm before serving.

## Notes

- No double boiler: melt the chocolate in the microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring after each.
- Best served chilled, or after softening at room temperature for 10 minutes.
- Better chocolate: swap the chips for about 12 oz chopped European bars, such as Lindt Excellence or Ritter Sport, or Moser Roth or Choceur from Aldi. Stir in about 1 tsp neutral oil or shortening per cup of chocolate for smoother dipping.
- Mix it up: coat batches in milk, dark, semisweet, or white chocolate, or drizzle one over another.
- Other flavors: butterscotch chips, mint or orange dark chocolate, or sea salt caramel. Flavored bars melt best without nuts or crunchy bits. Sprinkle flaky salt or crushed toffee on top while the coating is still wet.
- White chocolate and butterscotch scorch easily. Melt them over very low heat and stir often.
