# Peanut Butter Bars

**Makes:** 12 bars (9x13 pan) · **Prep:** 25 min · **Chill:** 1 hr
**Tags:** desserts, no-bake, bars
**Source:** Allrecipes

## Ingredients

### Base

- 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 2 cups confectioners' sugar
- 1 cup butter or margarine, melted
- 1 cup peanut butter

### Topping

- 1 1/2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
- 4 tbsp peanut butter

## Instructions

1. Mix the graham cracker crumbs, confectioners' sugar, melted butter, and 1 cup peanut butter in a medium bowl until well blended.
2. Press evenly into the bottom of an ungreased 9x13-inch pan.
3. Put the chocolate chips and 4 tbsp peanut butter in a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave on high, stirring every 15 seconds, until smooth.
4. Spread the chocolate mixture over the base.
5. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour, then cut into 12 squares.
