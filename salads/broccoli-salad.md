# Broccoli Salad

**Serves:** · **Prep:** · **Cook:**
**Tags:** salads
**Source:**

## Ingredients

### Salad

- Broccoli, chopped
- Red onion, diced
- Cheddar cheese
- Bacon bits
- Dried cranberries

### Dressing

- Mayo
- Vinegar
- Salt
- Pepper

## Instructions

1. Whisk the mayo, vinegar, salt, and pepper together.
2. Combine the broccoli, red onion, cheddar, bacon bits, and cranberries in a large bowl.
3. Toss with the dressing.
