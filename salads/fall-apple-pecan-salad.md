# Fall Apple Pecan Salad

**Serves:** 8-10 · **Prep:** · **Cook:** 5 min
**Tags:** salads, fall
**Source:**

## Ingredients

### Salad

- 10 cups mixed greens
- 2 Honeycrisp apples, thinly sliced
- 1 cup candied pecans (below)
- 1/2 cup dried cranberries
- 1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced

### Maple vinaigrette

- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### Candied pecans

- 1 cup pecan halves
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. For the pecans: melt the butter in a pan, then add the brown sugar, salt, and pecans. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring. Cool on parchment.
2. Whisk the vinaigrette ingredients together.
3. Combine the salad ingredients in a large bowl.
4. Toss with the dressing just before serving.
