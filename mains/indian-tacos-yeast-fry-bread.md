# Indian Tacos with Yeast Fry Bread

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 1 hr · **Cook:** 45 min
**Tags:** mains, fry-bread, tacos
**Source:** Allrecipes

## Ingredients

### Fry bread

- 1 (0.25 oz) package active dry yeast
- 1 tbsp white sugar
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 cup warm water
- 6 cups vegetable oil, for frying

### Chili

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 large onion, minced
- 1 (28 oz) can diced tomatoes, undrained
- 2 (6 oz) cans tomato paste
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and ground black pepper, to taste
- 1 pinch chili powder, or to taste

### Toppings

- 1/2 lb Cheddar cheese, coarsely shredded
- 1 head iceberg lettuce, cored and shredded
- 1 cup finely chopped onion, or as desired
- 1 cup finely chopped mild green chiles, or as desired

## Instructions

1. Mix the yeast, sugar, and 1/4 cup warm water in a large bowl. Let sit until a creamy foam forms on top, about 5 minutes.
2. Whisk the flour, 1 tsp salt, and baking powder in another bowl. Stir the flour mixture into the yeast mixture in thirds, alternating with the 1 cup warm water in thirds, and beat to make a firm dough.
3. Cut the dough into 6 pieces and roll each into a ball on a floured surface. Roll the balls out into flat rounds.
4. Heat the oil in a deep fryer or large saucepan to 350°F (175°C).
5. Fry the rounds one at a time, turning once, until golden brown on both sides, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Drain on paper towels.
6. For the chili: cook the ground beef and minced onion in a skillet over medium heat until browned and crumbly, about 10 minutes. Drain the grease.
7. Stir in the tomatoes with their juice, tomato paste, basil, oregano, salt, pepper, and chili powder. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer until thickened, 30 to 40 minutes.
8. To serve, put a piece of fry bread on a plate and ladle chili over it. Top with about 1 tbsp Cheddar and lettuce, onion, and green chiles as desired.

## Notes

- Shortcut: skip steps 1-3 and use store-bought refrigerated pizza dough (e.g. Harris Teeter dough balls). Let it sit at room temperature 20-30 minutes, divide into 6, and stretch thin. If it springs back, let it rest 5 minutes and try again.
- Canned biscuits (e.g. Grands) also work, flattened and fried. They come out sweeter and puffier.
