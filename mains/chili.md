# Boilermaker Tailgate Chili

**Serves:** 12 · **Prep:** 30 min · **Cook:** 2 hr
**Tags:** mains, big-batch, game-day
**Source:** [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/78299/boilermaker-tailgate-chili/)

## Ingredients

### Meat & beans

- 2 lb ground beef chuck
- 1 lb bulk Italian sausage
- 3 (15 oz) cans chili beans, drained
- 1 (15 oz) can chili beans in spicy sauce

### Tomatoes & liquids

- 2 (28 oz) cans diced tomatoes, with juice
- 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
- 1/2 cup beer
- 4 cubes beef bouillon

### Vegetables

- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 3 stalks celery, chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, seeded and chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, seeded and chopped
- 2 green chile peppers, seeded and chopped

### Seasonings

- 1/4 cup chili powder, or more to taste
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp bacon bits
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp hot pepper sauce (e.g. Tabasco)
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp salt, or more to taste
- 1 tsp ground black pepper, or more to taste
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp white sugar

### To serve

- 1 (10.5 oz) bag corn chips, such as Fritos
- 1 (8 oz) package shredded Cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat a large stock pot over medium-high heat. Crumble in the beef and sausage and cook, stirring often, until evenly browned. Drain off excess grease.
2. Add both kinds of beans, the diced tomatoes, and tomato paste.
3. Add the vegetables, bacon bits, bouillon, beer, and all the seasonings. Stir to blend.
4. Cover and simmer on low for at least 2 hours, stirring occasionally. The longer it simmers, the better.
5. Taste and add more salt, pepper, or chili powder if needed.
6. Ladle into bowls and top with corn chips and Cheddar.

## Notes

- Best made the night before: cool, refrigerate, and reheat the next day.
- Ground venison works in place of the ground beef. It's very lean, so there's little grease to drain. Brown it with a tablespoon of oil if it sticks.
