# Fry Bread Tacos

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 20 min · **Cook:** 22 min · **Rest:** 5 min
**Tags:** mains, fry-bread, tacos
**Source:** Allrecipes

## Ingredients

### Fry bread

- 2 cups all-purpose flour, or more as needed
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup milk
- Oil, for frying

### Filling

- 1 (15.5 oz) can pinto beans, with liquid
- 1/2 cup picante sauce, divided
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 (1.25 oz) package taco seasoning mix

### Toppings

- 2 cups shredded iceberg lettuce
- 1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Combine the beans and 2 tbsp of the picante sauce in a small saucepan over low heat. Cook until heated through, about 5 minutes.
2. Cook the ground beef with the taco seasoning in a large skillet over medium-high heat until browned, 5 to 8 minutes. Cover and keep warm.
3. For the fry bread: stir the flour, baking powder, and salt together in a medium bowl. Stir in the milk until the dough comes together, adding more flour if needed to handle it.
4. Knead on a floured surface until smooth, at least 5 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes.
5. Break off 3/4-cup pieces and shape into 1/4-inch-thick rounds, with a thinner dip in the center.
6. Heat 1 1/2 inches of oil in a large, deep, heavy skillet to 365°F (180°C). Fry the rounds until golden, about 3 minutes per side. Drain on paper towels.
7. Top the fry bread with beans, beef, lettuce, and Cheddar. Spoon the remaining picante sauce on top.

## Notes

- Make everything but the fry bread ahead, then reheat before serving.
- Other good toppings: onion, sour cream, guacamole.
- Leftover fry bread makes a dessert: coat with cinnamon and sugar and drizzle with honey.
