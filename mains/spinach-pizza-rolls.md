# Homemade Spinach Pizza Rolls

**Serves:** 8 · **Prep:** 20 min · **Cook:** 25 min
**Tags:** mains, appetizers, pizza
**Source:** Allrecipes

## Ingredients

- Non-stick aluminum foil, for lining the pan
- 1 tbsp cornmeal
- 8 oz bulk Italian sausage
- 1 (13.8 oz) package refrigerated pizza dough
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
- 1 cup fresh baby spinach
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 1 (8 oz) can pizza sauce, warmed

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a large baking sheet with non-stick foil and sprinkle with the cornmeal.
2. Brown the sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Drain.
3. Unroll the dough onto the baking sheet and stretch or roll it into a 13x10-inch rectangle.
4. Spread the sausage over the dough to within 1/2 inch of the edges. Sprinkle with 1/2 cup mozzarella, layer on the spinach, then top with the remaining 1/2 cup mozzarella and the Parmesan.
5. Starting from a long side, roll up into a 13-inch loaf. Pinch the seam and ends to seal.
6. Place seam side down on the baking sheet. Brush with the egg and cut four 2-inch slits in the top.
7. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until golden brown. Cover for the last 5 minutes if it's browning too fast.
8. Slice into rounds and serve with the warm pizza sauce.

## Notes

- Works as an appetizer, a side, or a weeknight dinner with a salad.
