# Bánh Mì Chả Gà (Chicken Patty Bánh Mì)

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 20 min + overnight rest · **Cook:** 15 min
**Tags:** mains, sandwich, vietnamese, make-ahead
**Source:** Homemade, from my former girlfriend, a phenomenal cook

## Ingredients

### Chicken patty (make 1 day ahead)

- 1 lb ground chicken (or swap up to 1/4 lb for peeled, deveined shrimp)
- 1 shallot, peeled
- 3 garlic cloves, peeled
- 1 tsp teriyaki sauce
- 1 tsp olive oil, plus a little more for the pan
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp baking soda

### Soy dipping sauce

- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sweet and sour sauce

### Vegetables

- 1 cucumber
- 1–2 carrots
- 1 bunch green onions
- 1–2 tbsp lemon juice
- A little oil, for searing the green onions

### To assemble

- 4 French bread rolls, or 1 large baguette cut into 4 pieces
- Mayonnaise

## Instructions

### The day before

1. Put the shallot and garlic in a small food processor or blender (a Ninja works great) and pulse until very finely minced. Don't let it turn into a paste.
2. In a bowl, combine the shallot and garlic with the ground chicken, teriyaki sauce, olive oil, salt, sugar, and baking soda. If using shrimp, pulse it in the processor until finely chopped and add it now.
3. Stir firmly in one direction for a minute or two, until the mixture looks sticky and holds together.
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight.

### The day of

5. **Make the sauce:** Stir the soy sauce and sweet and sour sauce together in a small bowl. Set aside.
6. **Prep the carrots:** Peel the carrots and cut into thin matchsticks (or thin slices). Toss with the lemon juice and let sit while you do everything else, at least 15 minutes.
7. **Prep the cucumber:** Cut into thin slices or long thin spears.
8. **Sear the green onions:** Chop the green onions. Heat a little oil in a small skillet over medium-high heat, add the onions, and cook 30–60 seconds, stirring, just until bright green, wilted, and fragrant. Scrape into a small bowl with any oil left in the pan.
9. **Cook the patty:** Heat a little olive oil in a large nonstick skillet (10–12 inch) over medium heat. Add the chicken mixture and spread into an even layer about 1/2 inch thick, like one big omelet. Cook 5–6 minutes, until the bottom is golden brown.
10. Flip it. The easiest way is to cut it into halves or quarters with a spatula and turn each piece, or slide it onto a plate and invert it back into the pan. Cook another 4–5 minutes, until the second side is browned and the center is cooked through (165°F).
11. Move to a cutting board, rest a couple of minutes, then slice into strips that fit the bread.
12. **Toast the bread:** Split the rolls lengthwise without cutting all the way through. Toast in a 400°F oven or toaster oven for 3–5 minutes, until the crust is crisp and the inside is warm.
13. **Assemble:** Spread mayonnaise on both cut sides of the bread. Add, in this order:
    - A spoonful of seared green onions
    - Two spoonfuls of the soy sauce mixture, drizzled over
    - Chicken patty strips
    - Carrots, drained of extra lemon juice
    - Cucumber
14. Press closed and eat right away while the bread is still crisp.

## Notes

- Don't skip the baking soda. It gives the patty the springy, bouncy texture of Vietnamese chả.
- The overnight rest lets the garlic and shallot flavor the meat and helps the texture set. If you're short on time, give it at least a few hours.
- The sauce is salty, so go light at first and add more to taste.
- Leftover patty keeps in the fridge for 3–4 days. Reheat briefly in a skillet before building sandwiches.
